Can Any Creator Pay to Go to Cannes? Here's How It Really Works

Why money alone won't get you on the red carpet, and what actually does.

By Bhavika Valecha, Founder, Affluent India PR · PR and reputation content creator and educator

Every year, photos of Indian creators on the Cannes red carpet fill our feeds. And every year, the same question comes up: can anyone who pays simply go?

The short answer is no. That's not how it works.

Money alone won't get you in

At Affluent India PR, we've worked with creators who have walked the red carpet at the Cannes Film Festival, in 2024 and again in 2025. From that experience, I can tell you there's a much stricter selection process behind it than most people think.

Yes, there is a fee involved. But money alone won't get you there.

What actually gets looked at

Your background, your body of work, your profile, your content and the kind of audience you speak to are all part of the decision. There's a proper multi-stage review process behind it, in both India and France.

That's why follower count isn't the deciding factor. Even a creator with millions of followers can be turned down if their positioning doesn't align with what Cannes is looking for.

Representation is intentional

The selection is also deliberate about representation. If ten creators are going from India, they won't all come from the same space. Fashion, beauty, entrepreneurship, art, cinema and social impact each bring a different story to the carpet.

So visibility at this level is never only about popularity. It's about relevance, positioning, and the story you represent.

Why PR and positioning matter

This is exactly why PR and positioning matter so much in this industry. Opportunities at this level are rarely built overnight. They come from a clear, consistent story about who you are, what you create and why your voice matters, built over time.

If you're a creator with Cannes on your vision board, now you know what really counts. Start with your positioning, and the right opportunities follow.
