Have You Ever Googled Yourself? Why It Can Make or Break You

Why what people find about you online decides whether they trust you.

By Bhavika Valecha, Founder, Affluent India PR · PR and reputation content creator and educator

Have you ever Googled yourself? If not, do it right now, and see whether you even show up.

Because what people find when they search your name can make or break things for you.

Everyone is asking the same question

At Affluent India PR, we help founders and creators build their online identity. And in a world where everyone is trying to sell something, winning someone's trust has become really hard.

Everyone is quietly asking the same question: can I trust this person?

Most of the time, you don't even get the chance to answer it yourself.

Google answers it for you

That's exactly where Google comes in. Before a client, investor, brand or collaborator ever speaks to you, they search for you, and what they find answers the question for you.

It's not just SEO. It's positioning

Google search isn't only about SEO. It's about positioning.

Every article you're mentioned in, every interview you give and every opinion you put out into the world works as a testimony for you. Together, they tell people who you are before you ever get to.

It doesn't happen on its own

A strong search presence isn't an accident. It gets built on purpose, one story, interview and feature at a time.

So, what comes up when you Google yourself?

If you don't like the answer, or there isn't one yet, let's talk.
