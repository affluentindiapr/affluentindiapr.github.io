About

A boutique agency with a new‑age approach

Founded by Bhavika Valecha, award-winning PR professional and one of the first movers in creator PR in India.

Who we are

Affluent India PR is founded by Bhavika Valecha, an award-winning PR professional with a decade of experience in creator, brand, lifestyle, entertainment and startup PR.

Affluent India PR is a new-age boutique communications agency that creates and safeguards the reputation of brands and leaders. We're built for how reputation works today: across the press, creators, social media and communities, and we were among the first in India to offer PR for content creators. We work with a select group of clients, so each one gets a tailored strategy and senior, hands-on attention.

We specialise in reputation management, executive positioning, media relations and influencer engagement, brought together in a communications strategy tailored to each client. Our core strength lies in advertising and marketing, startups, content creators, food and hospitality, lifestyle and travel, and beyond.

What we stand for

We believe in credibility over hype. Every strategy we build is tailored to your goals and your story, and we're always honest about what PR can and can't do, because the reputations worth building are the ones that last.

Founder

Bhavika Valecha

Founder · Award-winning PR professional

Bhavika has spent a decade in PR, specialising in creator, brand, lifestyle, entertainment and startup communications. She is known for campaigns that raise a brand's visibility and connect with the audiences that matter to it.

She is also one of the first movers in PR for content creators in India, building creator PR at a time when few agencies saw creators as brands in their own right.

Alongside her client work, Bhavika is building a community around PR literacy. Through her content, she demystifies how PR and reputation really work, giving founders, creators and young professionals the kind of practical knowledge that is rarely shared openly.
