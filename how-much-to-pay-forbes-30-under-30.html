How Much Do You Need to Pay to Get Into Forbes 30 Under 30?

Why credibility, not cash, is what gets founders and creators on the list.

By Bhavika Valecha, Founder, Affluent India PR · PR and reputation content creator and educator

It's probably the question I'm asked most often: "How much does Forbes charge?" Or, "How much do I need to pay to get featured?"

As someone who runs a PR agency and has had multiple clients featured on Forbes India and Forbes Asia over the years, I want to clear this up once and for all.

Getting into Forbes isn't a transaction

A lot of people assume that getting into Forbes is simply a matter of paying for it. That's not how I've seen it work.

In fact, if it were just about paying, a lot more people would be on the list. Trust me, there are plenty of people willing to pay. But that's not what Forbes is looking for.

What the people on the list have in common

The founders and creators who get recognised usually already have something worth talking about. Maybe they've built something innovative. Maybe they've solved a real problem. Maybe they've created real impact. Or maybe they simply have a genuinely compelling story.

Forbes isn't looking for the best PR pitch. It's looking for people doing noteworthy work.

What PR can and can't do

This is where the role of PR is often misunderstood.

PR can help tell your story. PR can help position your story. PR can help make sure the right people see it.

But PR cannot create credibility where none exists.

Credibility is the common thread

That's what keeps Forbes 30 Under 30 one of the most respected recognitions in the world. Whether it's Forbes India, Forbes Asia, Forbes Europe or Forbes US, the common thread is credibility.

Visibility can be created. Credibility has to be earned.
