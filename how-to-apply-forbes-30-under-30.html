Applying for Forbes 30 Under 30? Here's What I'd Focus On

Four things to focus on before you apply, from a PR founder whose clients have made the list.

By Bhavika Valecha, Founder, Affluent India PR · PR and reputation content creator and educator

Every year when Forbes 30 Under 30 nominations open, my inbox fills up with the same question: how do I give myself the best chance?

I've been in PR for over ten years, and I've had multiple clients make it to the Forbes 30 Under 30 list. So if I were applying, this is what I would focus on.

1. Don't start with PR. Start with your story

Before you think about press or visibility, get clear on the substance. What have you actually built? What have you achieved? What makes your work different from everyone else in your space?

2. Know your numbers

If you're a founder, be clear about the numbers that show the scale of your work: revenue, growth, customers, funding, team size, whatever applies to you.

If you're not a founder, think about the results and the impact you've created in your field, and how you can show them.

3. Document everything

Keep a record of your milestones, launches, awards, partnerships, media features and big wins. When you apply, you want to be able to show your journey and your impact clearly, not reconstruct it from memory.

4. Build your visibility

Talk about your work. Share your expertise. Build your personal brand. Get your work out there, so that the people reviewing your application can see it for themselves.

But remember: PR can amplify a strong story. It can't create one.

Make your application tell a clear story

If you're applying this year, make sure your application tells a clear story about what you've built, the impact you've created, and why your work stands out.
