Questions

Good to know

Can you guarantee a place on Forbes 30 Under 30?

No, and no one honestly can. Selection is entirely Forbes' editorial decision. What we do is make sure your story is strong, visible and presented at its best.

Can you buy a spot on the list?

No. A place on Forbes 30 Under 30 is earned through real achievements. Our founder Bhavika explains this in one of her most-watched reels.

Who is it for?

Founders, creators and young professionals under 30 who are creating real impact in their space, and want their work recognised beyond India, across Asia and globally.

When should I start?

The earlier, the better. Recognition like this builds on a track record, so the strongest nominations come from people who already have steady PR and visibility behind them. If you're starting fresh, begin at least several months before nominations open.
